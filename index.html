Nihar Shahani

I'm a photographer & videographer based in Mumbai, drawn to moments that feel honest, cinematic, and alive. Through stills and motion, I aim to capture not just how something looked, but how it felt. Whether behind a lens or behind an idea, my goal remains the same: to turn fleeting moments into stories worth remembering.

"If an image tells a thousand words and a video tells a thousand images, then I want to become the best storyteller I can be."
- Nihar Shahani
